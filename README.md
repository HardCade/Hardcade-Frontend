# Hardcade-Frontend
Frontend Multi émulateur Arcade &amp; Console

![VMG – V Modelines Generator](https://hardcade-system.com/img/img-accueil.png)

**A customizable frontend for arcade and retro gaming systems.**

Hardcade Frontend lets you organize and launch your games and emulators through a customizable interface, designed for arcade cabinets and retro gaming setups.

## Features

- **Resizable window:** adapt the interface to different display resolutions, from 240p CRT televisions to 1080p and higher on HD displays.
- **Customizable themes:** personalize the interface with themes, images, logos and visual settings.
- **720p themes:** additional themes designed for HD displays.
- **Emulator integration:** configure and launch your emulators from a unified interface.
- **Built-in Theme Editor:** customize the appearance of your frontend to suit your setup.
- **Configuration options:** adjust the frontend to your needs.
